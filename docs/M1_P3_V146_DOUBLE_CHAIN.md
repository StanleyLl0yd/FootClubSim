# M1 P3 v146 — two substitutions with a chained replacement

The v145 three-to-five replacement adapter permits a player who came on
as a substitute to be withdrawn again. The older v136 **exactly two**
adapter and its browser second-outgoing selector still accidentally
required a pre-match starter. This narrow fix adds the same physically
legal chained two-change case while preserving the existing normal
double-substitution route unchanged.

The source-replayed v105/v120 engine still verifies the active XI,
available bench, referee-authorized 300-second checkpoints, legal
goalkeeper and foreigner caps, five normal replacements and three
in-play windows. Regression covers two ordered commands, illegal IDs,
duplicate incoming, reversed time, source replay, native Chrome/Edge
IndexedDB CAS and tenth-match reload. No invented medical/referee/KDK
authority, no M0/v8 persistence or production RNG changes, no 2024/25
holdout access, no historical score matching. P3/P5/M1 and exit audits
remain OPEN; P4 deferred, M2 prohibited.
