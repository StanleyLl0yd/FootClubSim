# M1 P5 v125 — observed 100-second playable-source pilot

This opt-in eight-shard workflow runs the real v123 driver against the
separate source-replayed schema-120 playable career, after the v122
sequential match implementation and v123 statistical instrument are merged.
It is not the isolated v31/v99/v104 research generator or the capped v8 source.

- Eight disjoint fixed-seed groups: 1–8, 9–16, …, 57–64.
- Four scheduled controlled-club fixtures per seed, **256 planned fixture
  slots**, not a claim that 256 real jobs or accepted matches have completed.
- A failed career stops at the failing fixture. Every actually attempted
  fixture and its rejection reason remains in the denominator; future
  unattempted fixtures are not silently counted as successes.
- Each shard prints its exact checked-out commit SHA, full JSON aggregate,
  SHA-256 of its JSON line, accepted/rejected counts, source replay counts,
  home/draw/away, goals, shots, shots on target and open-play-only xG.
- Verify actual workflow jobs, SHA, all eight seed ranges, checksums and
  denominators **after** the workflow runs; do not infer results from this
  document, synthetic unit tests or a green unrelated workflow.
- The push trigger is path-filtered and fires on the initial guarded merge.
  The dispatch trigger supports a repeatable manual observation.

Modelled referee/cards/clinical injuries are absent from the ordinary-only
v123 source and MUST NOT be reported as zero observed real-world incidents.
Shot taxonomy and all-goal subtype definitions are not source-harmonized
with the independent RPL 2025/26 development aggregate. A sample of 256
planned games cannot establish independent calibration, release performance,
full opponent history, or clustered confidence intervals. Match outcomes
must never be tuned to historical exact scores.
