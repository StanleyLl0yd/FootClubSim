# Dated RFU Cup fixture source coverage: all 16 participants

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-14. **Scope:** links and match dates for eight **completed** 4–5 August 2026 FONBET Cup first-round matches, two distinct teams each, covering all **16/16** approved «Лига приколов» club IDs. This is **not** eight frozen RPL registrations, a full first-team roster or a right to import an external database.

These historical fixture pages on the [RFU's official Cup competition site](https://www.rfs.ru/cup/tournament/matches?TournamentMatchesFilter%5Bdate%5D=before) are a reliable source *discovery path* for independently checking that a named person appeared on a particular date. Each page has a specific fixture, teams and archived match account. The site may serve stale cached "not started" states for an unfiltered link; a reader must confirm the finalized match state, date, teams and source timestamp rather than assume that all response variants are synchronized. In particular, a **completed match page is not a signed club-wide registration certificate**.

## Critical time and rights distinction

- 4–5 August was **before** the end of the 2026 summer transfer registration window. The match pages are earlier than multiple reported September moves, so they **cannot be reused as 14 October memberships**. Example: the [5 August Zenit / Baltika fixture](https://www.rfs.ru/cup/tournament/match/57198) predates the already recorded August/September departure leads in [Nadir's federation index cross-check](NADIR_RFS_CUP_CROSSCHECK.md).
- Cup and RPL are **different competitions**: Cup eligibility is not automatically RPL registration/foreigner eligibility, and a specific match appearance is not an exhaustive squad denominator.
- No permission is assumed for automatic bulk collection, copying third-party player/club databases, public offline packaging, name/likeness use, statistics, emblems or photos. This document verifies source discovery only.
