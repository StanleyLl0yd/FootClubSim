## Method and limitations

- Scope: 16 exact Russian display names from `src/branding/identities.ts` (each prefixed with a football-club query such as `"ФК Изумруд" футбол`), manually inspected in public web search on 2026-10-14. Where a compound multi-name query produced no relevant result, specific-name queries were used. Search results can be absent, unindexed, stale, ambiguous or irrelevant.
- **Not performed:** formal FIPS/Rospatent, TMview/EUIPO, WIPO, territorial company-name or domain clearance, archived trade-name history for every region, phonetic/Latin transliteration similarity, stylized-mark comparison, confusion likelihood assessment or legal opinion. No absence of search hits proves uniqueness, abandonment, registrability or freedom to use a mark.
- Art: the project-authored `ClubBadge.tsx` uses text and three concentric circles driven by local hexadecimal colors, with no downloaded graphics. This is **source-level authorship evidence**, not visual clearance against existing crests, trade dress, club color arrangements or official identity associations. Re-review the actual production artwork and game/website/store presentation before shipping.
