# M1 P3 v101 - source-bound physical XI at intraphase shot slots

Opt-in **schema 101** is the first narrow bridge from the replay-verified
v8 300-second physical-availability/medical events into the versioned v99
100-second shot-opportunity clock. It does **not** change v8/v99 outputs,
legacy source RNG, M0, saved careers, production match simulation, or
schema-6/v76 CAS. It is an isolated prototype, not accepted gameplay.

The v8 replay first validates the original prematch/actor authority,
normal substitutions, cautions, red dismissals, withdrawal events and
ordinary outfield temporary medical exits/returns using the existing
source contracts. Only the verified availability and medical transitions
are projected, preserving their exact checkpoint order, identities,
and source records. For each 300-second interval, those transitions
update the legal physical on-field XI **before** three 100-second v99
shot opportunities are generated with a separate deterministic v101
shot RNG. Players absent for treatment, dismissed, withdrawn or substituted
out cannot be selected as shooters in any subsequent subslot.
The full result, including source transition records, player attribution,
shooting outcomes, clock, scores and RNG, is checked by full deterministic
replay. A zero-transition fixture must preserve the exact v99 shot
report, draw count (270) and RNG continuation.

**Boundaries:** the underlying medical and substitution transitions remain
limited to existing 300-second v8 checkpoints. There is no admitted
physical transition at arbitrary 100-second shot times, no referee
stoppage/restart model at those times, no external referee, physician
or KDK identity authentication, no penalties or own-goal source, and
no authenticated injury diagnosis or clinical recovery. v101 explicitly
rejects custom tactical/player-focus commands and does not accept
externally asserted adjudications. In particular the v8 referee signal
field is *not* proof of a real official decision. Source-v8 replay
is a deterministic **internal rules gate**, not independent third-party
authority evidence. No v101 match may be admitted to existing v76
career CAS. No new frequency, accuracy or xG release calibration is
claimed; an untouched independent season holdout is still required.

This narrow integration reduces P3's source-clock gap, but does **not**
complete P3 #29 or P5 #31. M1 OPEN, M2 prohibited, P4 #30 deferred.
M1 exit audit #3 still requires two complete repository-wide passes.
