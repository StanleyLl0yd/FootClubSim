# M1 P3: late normal substitution, short-handed XI, and 60 running seconds

**IFAB 2026/27 Law 3, official [time-limited substitution
protocol](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/time-limited-substitution-protocol/).
Isolated versioned v13 RESEARCH STATE MACHINE, NOT integrated match engine.**

## Verified sporting rule

A player named for an ordinary substitution is to leave within ten
seconds of the final applicable referee board/signal. If the outgoing
player fails to leave within the time limit and no injury/security
exception applies, the player **still leaves**; the nominated replacement
must not enter, the substitution cannot be cancelled or changed, and play
restarts. After **one full minute of running match time** has elapsed from
that restart, the substitute may enter at the **first subsequent
stoppage** with a referee signal. The late player is not automatically
cautioned simply for crossing the ten-second threshold: a caution
requires excessive delay. Safety, injury and near-the-line exceptional
referee rulings are outside this narrow entrypoint.

## Implemented and tested

`src/core/m1-v13-late-entry-physical-xi.ts` represents a *single*
ordinary **outfield** pending change after a **seed-verified opening
goal restart**:

- Validates the original M1 prematch, fixture, starter/bench IDs and
  prospective ordinary legal eligibility by replaying the current v8
  simulator for admission **without ever using that hypothetical
  eleven-player simulation's future goals or shots**.
- Checks the preceding valid goal is generated by the same RNG seed in
  the actual original fixture (e.g. t=299, restart t=300).
- When the late departure is recorded, the outgoing player is **actually
  removed** from the physical XI. The named incoming player stays on
  the bench; the team is down to ten. Their IDs are locked.
- `playing` actions advance **only running game time**, not an
  alleged wall-clock pause; sub-minute `stoppage` reports below 60
  running seconds cannot admit the substitute.
- The first *reported* qualifying stoppage at t >= restart + 60
  requires a separately declared referee signal. Without it the
  transition is refused, leaving the pending state intact. After
  admission the same XI returns to eleven with the *originally named*
  substitute, and replay refuses further mutation.
- The whole bounded 32-step action history is **re-derived from
  externally supplied original prematch, policy and locked procedure**
  before each accepted transition. Tampered substitutions, changed
  restart timing, fabricated XI, event chronology, forged history,
  getters and cycles are rejected.
- Both home and away team physical presence, absent-player eligibility
  and source replay are unit-tested.

### Critical nonclaims

The `stoppage` and `refereeSignalId` messages after the short-handed
restart are **NOT authenticated**; the state reports
`subsequentWhistleAuthorityAuthenticated:false` and
`refereeSignalAuthorityAuthenticated:false`. This protocol **cannot
certify** a legal real-world or production virtual referee entry until
a future in-game referee generates those events itself.

Most importantly the actual historical v8 shot phase loop is **NOT
run with ten players** after the late exit. It would be wrong to
recycle goals precomputed for eleven players. Therefore every state
reports `downstreamShotsRecomputedForShortHandedXI:false` and is
**not** a simulated match or a new saved career. The next engine
implementation must directly feed this XI state into the phase scorer
and generate legitimate subsequent stoppages from that *same dynamic
playing state*. Only that integrated version can count toward complete
P3 gameplay acceptance.

The simulation of a goalkeeper's late replacement, a group containing
a delayed exit, certified referee injury/safety exceptions and
competition-specific extraordinary substitution opportunities remains
unsupported; these require separate IFAB/source-verified tests.
The bounded research scenario models a normal substitution after an
actual seeded goal only, not every way a physical match may stop.
