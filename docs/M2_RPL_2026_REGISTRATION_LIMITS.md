# M2 RPL 2026/27: bounded player-registration and on-field limit checks

**Status: source-described arithmetic only; NO verified registrations and NO new playable pack.** This is an independent future-M2 module, not a change to the accepted v188 synthetic match/season, old careers, snapshots, migrations or the public UI.

Official first-party rules reviewed: [RFU 2026/27 RPL competition regulation, clauses 5.8–5.8.1, PDF p. 17](https://www.rfs.ru/subject/1/documents/preview?documentId=2164), with [project source notes](RPL_2026_27_RULES.md). The model checks at most 25 registrations on list 3A, 30 on 3B, 50 on 3C; 3B births not earlier than 2005-01-01; 3C births not earlier than 2007-01-01 and explicitly established RFU national-team eligibility; at most 12 combined 3A+3B legionnaires, at least two 3A+3B goalkeepers, up to two inter-RPL loan players, and up to seven verified legionnaires among one team's 11 starters. It tracks at least 12 homegrown 3A entries with at least four club-trained and eight association-trained claims.

The RFU document describes a **reduced registration quota** if required homegrown players are missing, but this adapter **does not guess the formula or approve a deficient list**. It returns `HOMEGROWN_QUOTA_ADJUSTMENT_UNRESOLVED` pending precise implementation. It never infers legionnaire status from a passport or nationality, nor infers football age eligibility from an unverified birth date: `null` facts become explicit blockers. Inconsistent homegrown claims, duplicate fictional person IDs, invalid leap dates and unknown starting players fail validation.

**Trust boundary:** input facts are caller assertions. The checker has no original registration, document signatures, official athlete transfers, temporary loans, medical certificates, federation approvals, first-aid/CPR certificates, qualification history, disciplinary KDK rulings or proof of source redistribution rights. Even a result with `numericalLimitsPass:true` explicitly reports `registrationAuthenticated:false` or `matchEligibilityAuthenticated:false`. It is **not** evidence that any real RPL individual was registered or that any actual match XI was permitted. No provider data or personal information is bundled.
