# M1 P3 v118 — deterministic fictional return after internally generated injury

An internal *fictional* referee in v19 may generate a modelled
`injury-withdrawal` command. This is **not** a diagnosis or a signed
real medical clearance. The v118 opt-in browser career projection can assign
1–4 missed **fully recorded controlled-club fixtures** after such an incident,
using a deterministic domain-separated hash of fixture, incident and player
identifiers. These are uncalibrated engineering gameplay durations, not
real-world recovery estimates.

Before allowing any modelled return, the projector reconstructs the entire
v19 source generator output from the immutable catalog and that fixture's
replay-validated match source. It requires exact equality of **every** planned
command, and no medical-action or external-decision injections. An event ID,
`trusted-medical` field, or isolated source assertion by itself never
authorizes recovery. All unmatched medical holds remain indefinite. Known
straight-red/KDK holds and opponent incomplete histories remain conservative
and are never automatically cleared.

A newly recorded source injury is excluded for every missed fixture,
including the named bench; the next complete controlled-club fixture
after that period may select the recovered player. The browser shows the
fictional return and its absence count, without falsely claiming
authentic medical clearance. The old v115 projection remains callable
unchanged and indefinitely withholds all medical withdrawals; the new
v118 projection is explicitly versioned and opt-in. M0, v8 journal source
formats, historic events, CAS transaction and RNG are **unchanged**.

Regression tests cover a bounded fixed-seed search for an actual verified
modelled injury, exclusion over the required completed matches, re-admission,
continued journal replay, attempted forged sources and M0 immutability.

Limitations: no diagnosed severity, independent medical evidence, official
suspension resolution, full opponent season or statistical injury/recovery
calibration. P3, P5 and M1 remain OPEN. Holdout 2024/25 is untouched;
M2 prohibited, P4 deferred; exit audit #3 remains 0/2.
