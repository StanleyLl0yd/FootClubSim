## Targeted verification against the primary IFAB 2026/27 text

Source edition: official [IFAB 2026/27 Law 3, The Players, §§3.1-3.5](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-players/); official [2026/27 Time-limited substitution protocol](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/time-limited-substitution-protocol/); official [2026/27 change register](https://www.theifab.com/law-changes/latest/). These directly accessible **IFAB primary pages** are stronger than an RFU press announcement for the clauses listed here. The `latest` path is **not revision-immutable**: freeze the **2026/27 edition label and review date**, record actual text/version references for future executable packs, and re-check on a future season transition.
